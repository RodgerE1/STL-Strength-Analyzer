The downloaded orientation-export example was reloaded and retained
44 triangles, a closed single shell, and its 7,680 mm³ volume.

Large-model check:
A 199,800-triangle closed cylinder was analyzed in approximately 9 seconds
on the development environment. Its volume was 75,397.73 mm³ versus
75,398.22 mm³ for the ideal smooth cylinder; the small difference is expected
from faceting. These timings are not a benchmark of the user's PC.

These checks validate software geometry cases and interface behavior.
They do not validate a printed part's physical strength or a failure load.
